Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go. You can permanently delete your BARZ Battle League account and all associated data using either of the methods below.

Option 1: Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open the BARZ Battle League app on your phone.
  2. Tap your Profile icon (bottom right).
  3. Tap the Settings (gear) icon.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete Account".
  5. Confirm by entering your password (or re-authenticating with Google).
  6. Your account, verses, posts, messages, votes, and profile photo will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access to the app, email us at:

📧 support@dropbarz.app
Subject line: Account Deletion Request

Include the following in your email:

  • Your registered email address
  • Your username (if you remember it)
  • The reason for deletion (optional)

We will process your request within 7 business days and confirm via email.

What data is deleted?

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed within 30 days:

  • Your profile (username, email, photo, bio, date of birth)
  • Your verses, audio recordings, and battle entries
  • Your direct messages and voice room participation history
  • Your comments, votes, and reactions
  • Your push notification tokens
  • Authentication credentials (password hash / Google linkage)

What data may be retained?

We may retain limited anonymized data:

  • For legal compliance, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution (max 12 months).
  • Aggregated, non-identifiable analytics that cannot be tied back to you.

Questions?
